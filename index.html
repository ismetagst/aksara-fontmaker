From a first stroke to a typeface.

A simple workflow, room to make it your own.

Draw your letters.

Start freehand, build shapes with the pen tool, or upload a scanned sketch to trace. Work through numbers, uppercase, and lowercase at your own pace.

Find the right curve.

Move anchors, adjust their handles, and set each character's advance width. Use guides and zoom to get the details feeling right.

Type it. Keep it.

Try your letters together in the live preview. Give your font a name, then download an installable .otf file to use beyond the editor.
